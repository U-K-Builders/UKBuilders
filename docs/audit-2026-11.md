# Website audit, November 2026 (Phase 1)

Audited on 12 November 2026: the live site (https://uk-builders-three.vercel.app,
`main` at `2701649`) and `ui` at `8169de9`. Since today's release they are the
**same build** (identical JavaScript and CSS files), so every finding applies to
both. Nothing in the site code was changed by this audit.

## Summary

The site looks premium, the lead pipeline is secure, and Lighthouse rates
accessibility, best practices and SEO highly. But several things stand between
a visitor and an enquiry:

1. **The forms ask for the wrong things.** The Contact form makes email and a
   message compulsory and phone optional; the site-visit form demands email.
   Most buyers would rather give a phone number.
2. **On phones set to dark mode, the main buttons are unreadable** (cream
   text on gold, 1.7:1 contrast): "Explore current projects", the gold
   "Enquire" in the bottom bar, "View details", "Send message".
3. **On phones narrower than 375px (including common 360px Androids) the
   page slides sideways and the menu button is partly or fully off-screen.**
4. **Legal and trust gaps:** no MahaRERA QR codes, no privacy policy, no
   "artist's impression" labels on renders, an unregistered project (Elvira)
   offered for visits and "investment", hidden projects in form dropdowns, and
   several numbers and promises nobody has verified.
5. **It is slow on a typical phone:** the main content appears after 4.7 to
   7.1 seconds on slow 4G (target: under 3 s). Mobile performance scores 65
   to 80 (target 90+).

Fix those five and the site will earn noticeably more enquiries. The rest of
this document lists every finding, ranked by how much it costs enquiries.

## Findings, ranked by impact on enquiries

Owner codes: **A** feature/navigation-design · **B** feature/trust ·
**C** feature/enquiry-flow · **D** feature/security-speed · **Foundation**
(shared clean-up that the others build on).

### 1. Forms ask for email and a message, not a phone number — C

- **What:** Contact form: name, **email (required)**, phone (optional),
  "interested in", **message (required)**. "Book a site visit": name, phone
  **and email**, all required. "Book a video call": name, email, phone, date,
  time slot, all required. Investor, redevelopment, contractor and career
  forms all require email too. Only the "Register interest" pop-up on project
  pages asks for just name + phone.
- **Where:** `src/pages/ContactPage.jsx:14-27`, `src/pages/ProjectDetailPage.jsx:266-276`,
  `src/pages/VirtualCallPage.jsx:61-77`, `src/pages/InvestorsPage.jsx:70-74`.
- **Why it matters:** many buyers (especially older ones) don't use email, and
  every extra required field loses people. A phone number is what the sales
  team needs to call back.
- **Also:** error messages are 12px red text with 3.5–3.8:1 contrast (fails
  WCAG 1.4.3) and aren't linked to their field for screen readers (except in
  the Register-interest pop-up). "Phone" accepts 7–15 characters on most forms
  but 7–20 on the video-call form and in the API.
- **Fix:** one shared form: **name, phone (required), optional message**, plus
  the project when known. Same rules and messages everywhere, errors at 16px
  in dark red, announced to screen readers. Make "Book a site visit" the same
  form with a preset project.

### 2. In dark mode the main gold buttons are unreadable — A

- **What:** the site follows the phone's dark setting by default. In dark mode
  the rule `.dark .text-navy { color: #e8dfc8 }` also repaints the navy text on
  gold buttons, giving cream on gold (**1.66:1**; needs 4.5:1).
- **Where:** `src/index.css:186`; affects the hero "Explore current projects",
  the gold "Enquire" in the mobile bottom bar, "View details", "Send message",
  "Register interest". Red "Sold out" badges drop to 2.5:1.
- **Why it matters:** a large share of phones run in dark mode, so many
  visitors see the key call-to-action buttons as blank gold bars.
- **Fix (simplest, rule 2):** remove dark mode, which is a second colour scheme
  to maintain for little buyer value. Or keep it and exclude gold buttons from
  the override. Remove the second dark-mode switch in the accessibility panel
  either way.

### 3. Small phones: sideways scrolling and a hidden menu button — A

- **What:** the header row needs 374px (logo + name + three icon buttons). On
  any screen narrower than 375px the whole page scrolls sideways and the menu
  button is cut off: partly at 360px, mostly at 344px, and **completely
  off-screen at 320px and 280px**.
- **Where:** `src/components/Header.jsx:179-222` (logo text `shrink-0`, three
  44px icon buttons).
- **Why it matters:** 360px is one of the most common Android widths in India,
  and the release notes promise the site works from 280px. Measured on the
  live site after today's release.
- **Fix:** drop the accessibility and dark-mode buttons from the header (see
  findings 2 and 9) or move them into the menu, and let the name shrink or
  hide below 360px. Test at 280/320/360.
- **Also at 768px (iPad portrait):** on Christina the "2 BHK + 2 BHK"
  floor-plan title overflows its card and the page (`whitespace-nowrap`,
  `src/pages/ProjectDetailPage.jsx:493`) and "Sold out" badges overlap titles.

### 4. Call and WhatsApp are not always one tap away — C

- **What:**
  - The Call / WhatsApp / Enquire bar exists only on phones (below 768px, and
    not on phones held sideways). On tablets, laptops and landscape phones there
    is **no Call or WhatsApp button** anywhere on screen; only a gold
    "Enquire" link.
  - The Contact page has no WhatsApp link at all, and lists the phone number
    last, after the whole form.
  - In the footer, the phone number and email are **plain text, not links**
    (`src/components/Footer.jsx:103`).
  - Project pages have no Call / WhatsApp buttons. "Request floor plan" and the
    shop "Enquire now" buttons are `mailto:` links
    (`src/pages/ProjectDetailPage.jsx:471, 508`), which open an email app or
    nothing.
- **Why it matters:** NRI buyers often browse on laptops and use WhatsApp; a
  65-year-old on a tablet can't find a number to tap.
- **Fix:** a Call + WhatsApp pair in the header from 768px up, and on every
  project page next to the RERA number. Phone and WhatsApp first on the Contact
  page. Make every printed number a `tel:` link. Replace `mailto:` buttons with
  WhatsApp ("Send me the Justin floor plans") or the shared form.

### 5. MahaRERA compliance on the page is incomplete — B

- **What:**
  - **No MahaRERA QR codes** anywhere (issue #51). MahaRERA requires the QR
    code to be shown with the registration number in every advertisement.
  - RERA numbers appear only inside each project page's stats bar. They are not
    on the home page, the Ongoing Projects cards, or the footer, and there is no
    mention of the MahaRERA website (maharera.maharashtra.gov.in).
  - Building images for Justin and Christina are **renders**, shown without an
    "artist's impression" label (hero, cards, gallery, share image).
  - No disclaimer (images indicative, specifications subject to RERA-registered
    plans, etc.).
- **Where:** `src/components/HeroSection.jsx`, `src/components/ProjectCard.jsx`,
  `src/pages/ProjectDetailPage.jsx:403-415`, `src/pages/GalleryPage.jsx:6-7`,
  `src/components/Footer.jsx`.
- **Why it matters:** this is a legal requirement and the first thing a careful
  buyer checks. The home page claims "RERA compliant" without showing a
  number.
- **Fix:** a small `ReraBadge` (number + QR + "Verify on MahaRERA") on every
  project card, project page and the footer; "Artist's impression" on renders;
  a short disclaimer in the footer. Needs the QR images from Avi.

### 6. Unregistered and hidden projects are advertised — B

- **What:**
  - **Elvira** has no RERA number but has a full public page
    (`/projects/elvira`, shows "RERA: Pending registration", floor plans,
    "Book a site visit" form), a "Pre-launch waitlist" on `/projects/upcoming`
    (hidden from the menu but reachable), a card on the Investors page as a
    "current investment opportunity", and a pin on the home-page map.
  - **New Life and Madonna** are marked `hidden`, yet appear in the Contact
    form dropdown and the Book-a-video-call dropdown.
- **Where:** `src/data/projects.js:24-57`, `src/data/projectDetails.js:230-310`,
  `src/pages/ContactPage.jsx:4-12`, `src/pages/VirtualCallPage.jsx:5`,
  `src/pages/InvestorsPage.jsx:195`, `src/pages/HomePage.jsx:66-70`.
- **Why it matters:** RERA forbids advertising or booking a project before
  registration. It also confuses buyers ("which projects are actually on
  sale?").
- **Fix:** show only registered projects anywhere public. Build every dropdown
  and list from the data file (registered projects only). Remove the
  Upcoming page and Elvira's public page until it is registered.

### 7. Numbers, claims and promises nobody has verified — B (promises: C)

None of these may be false, but none is confirmed in the handoff notes, and on
a property site an unverifiable claim costs trust (and, under RERA, can be an
offence). Each needs Avi's confirmation or removal:

- **Investors page** (`src/pages/InvestorsPage.jsx:5-44, 149`): "₹2 Cr+
  typical capital appreciation", "100% on-time delivery record", "zero project
  abandonment", rents "₹55,000 – ₹1.10 Lac per month", "gross yields 3.5–4.5%",
  "development pipeline of ₹500 Cr+", "small-format (14 storeys, ~100 units)",
  "Final units available" / "Limited inventory". Return or appreciation claims
  are risky in property advertising.
- **Home "RERA Compliant: All projects fully registered"**
  (`src/components/TrustPillars.jsx:28-29`): RERA dates from 2017, so most of
  the 50+ older projects were never registered. Say "Justin and Christina are
  MahaRERA registered" with the numbers instead.
- **Same amenity and finish lists for Justin and Christina**
  (`src/data/projectDetails.js:152-153, 197-198`): "Italian marble",
  "engineered hardwood", "double-glazed panes", "Legrand / Schneider",
  "State-of-the-art gym", "Vastu compliant". These must match the registered
  specifications for each building.
- **Progress:** Justin "60% construction complete", Christina "15%"; the
  progress steps still show "Q2 2026" as the current stage (issue #52). The
  "Site progress" section says "photos coming soon".
- **Testimonials:** 6 quotes with names but no project or date
  (`src/data/testimonials.js`). Treat as unverified until Avi confirms each one
  (with the buyer's permission) and its project.
- **Completed projects:** 33 descriptions use the same template ("branded
  furnishings", "timeless design"); 11 are listed under other builder names
  (Happy Home Builders, Shivam Builders, etc.) and 2 as joint ventures, yet
  some are called "our earliest recorded landmark".
- **Other pages:** Contractors "3 ongoing projects + 2 upcoming"
  (`src/pages/ContractorsPage.jsx:41`, there are 2 ongoing); Redevelopment
  "Zero legal risk", "in-house legal team", "20–35% larger flat at zero
  cost" (`src/pages/RedevelopmentPage.jsx:33-38`); Careers lists four open
  roles; History "cutting-edge technologies and eco-friendly practices".
- **Promises the site can't keep yet (C):** "We'll email the guide within 24
  hours" (no guides exist in the repo), "We'll notify you at each milestone…
  unsubscribe at any time" (no mailing system), "a Zoom link within 2 business
  hours", "get back to you within one business day". Each lead only creates a
  row and one alert email.

### 8. Slow on a typical phone — D

- **What (measured):** LCP 4.7–7.1 s, FCP 2.2–2.9 s, Time to Interactive about
  6.5 s on home and project pages. Causes, biggest first:
  1. **Large photos:** the hero/project photo is a 1379×2000 JPEG (313 KiB)
     even on a 375px phone; the Ongoing section loads both 313/319 KiB photos.
     No smaller sizes, no WebP/AVIF. Lighthouse estimates 556 KiB savings on
     home. The 40px logo is a 93 KiB PNG.
  2. **Map library on the home page:** MapLibre (270 KiB compressed, 1 MB
     unpacked) loads as soon as the home page opens, for a map far below the
     fold (`src/pages/HomePage.jsx:66`); project pages load it for two maps.
  3. **One bundle for all 17 pages:** 127 KiB compressed (466 KiB) of
     JavaScript; Lighthouse finds 270 KiB unused on first load. Every page is
     imported eagerly in `src/App.jsx`.
  4. **Render-blocking Google Fonts** (11 styles requested) plus a third-party
     connection before text shows.
  5. **No long-term caching:** `/assets/*` (hashed, never change) and
     `/images/*` are served with `max-age=0, must-revalidate`, so returning
     visitors re-check every file.
  6. The lead function runs in Washington DC (`iad1`) while the database is in
     Mumbai, adding latency to every form submission.
- **Fix:** generate 480/800/1200px WebP (and keep JPEG) in
  `scripts/build-images.mjs` and use `srcset`/`sizes`; a 2–4 KiB logo; load
  the map only when scrolled to (or replace the home map with a static image
  and a "Open in Google Maps" link); lazy-load routes; self-host the two fonts
  (2–3 weights) with `preload`; add `Cache-Control: public,
  max-age=31536000, immutable` for `/assets/(.*)` in `vercel.json`; set the
  function region to `bom1`.

### 9. Too many pages and options for a buyer — A

- **What:** 16 page routes and 5 top-level menus with 12 sub-items (Projects, About,
  Work With Us, Buyer Guides, Contact) plus an accessibility panel and a
  dark-mode button. Justin and Christina are not in the menu. Three overlapping
  "About" pages (Our Legacy, Company History, Project Timeline). Features with
  little buyer value: search box and BHK filters for two projects; colour-vision
  "filters" that *simulate* colour blindness (they don't help colour-blind
  users); a live seconds countdown; a gallery with three empty "coming soon"
  tabs; a time-zone booking form; "Download free guide" buttons with no guides;
  the hidden Upcoming page.
- **Taps:** from home, Justin is 2 taps via the hero button, but the "View
  details" button sits about 1,500px down the Ongoing page (below the search
  box and filters). Via the menu on a phone it is 4 taps.
- **Why it matters:** rule 2: every extra choice makes it harder for a
  first-time or older visitor to find a project and call.
- **Fix:** top menu: **Justin · Christina · Completed projects · About us ·
  Contact** (5 items, no dropdowns), Call/WhatsApp always visible. Fold
  Investors, Redevelopment, Contractors and Careers into short sections or one
  "Work with us" page linked from the footer. Remove the search/filters,
  colour filters, dark mode, seconds countdown and empty gallery tabs.

### 10. Text is small and hard to read — A

- **What:** measured share of visible text below 16px: home 40–47%, Ongoing
  69–71%, Contact 70–72%, project pages 82–86%; the typical paragraph is 14px.
  Labels are 12px uppercase with wide letter-spacing. Headings, the logo and
  testimonial quotes use Cinzel, an all-capitals display font (the quotes are
  set in fake italic capitals). Some paragraphs are justified.
- **Where:** site-wide Tailwind classes `text-xs`/`text-sm`; `src/index.css:41-56`;
  `src/components/TestimonialCard.jsx:4-7`.
- **Fix:** body 18px (minimum 16px) and labels 14px+ in sentence case via
  theme tokens; Cinzel only for large headings; left-aligned text.

### 11. Moving content that can't be stopped — A

- **What:**
  - The testimonial carousel slides every 5 seconds, **keeps moving when
    "reduce motion" is on**, and has **no pause button** (fails WCAG 2.2.2).
    Its dots are 8–10px (fails WCAG 2.5.8 target size); it marks itself as
    "tabs", and its cloned slides are read out twice by screen readers.
    (`src/components/Carousel.jsx:41, 134-150`)
  - The possession countdown ticks every second, also with reduced motion
    (`src/pages/ProjectDetailPage.jsx:16-53`).
  - A full-screen navy flash on every page change
    (`src/components/PageTransition.jsx`), a parallax project hero
    (`ProjectDetailPage.jsx:354-362`) and a slowly zooming home photo.
  - The 3D "Legacy projects" carousel is fine: pause button, arrow keys,
    no autoplay with reduced motion.
- **Fix:** testimonials as a simple static list (or the 3D carousel's
  behaviour); "Possession: August 2027" instead of a ticking clock; remove the
  page flash and parallax.

### 12. Tap targets below 48px — A

- **What:** 80–100% of tappable items are under 48px in at least one
  direction. Most are 44–46px (close), but real problems: testimonial dots
  8–10px; footer links 12px text with 12px gaps; Contact page phone and email
  links 16px tall; floor-plan and shop buttons 34px; filter buttons 44px; the
  "Back to top" button 40px; social icons 40px.
- **Fix:** a 48px minimum in the shared button and link styles.

### 13. No privacy policy or consent; data kept indefinitely — B (consent text: C)

- **What:** no privacy policy or disclaimer page (`/privacy` shows the
  "page not found" screen); no consent line on any of the 11 forms; no stated
  retention period. The India DPDP Act 2023 expects a clear notice of what is
  collected, why, for how long, and how to withdraw consent. Google Fonts and
  Carto map tiles also receive every visitor's IP address.
- **Fix:** a short plain-English Privacy page and Disclaimer page linked from
  the footer; one line under every form ("We'll use your number only to
  contact you about U.K. Builders homes. Privacy policy."); decide a retention
  period for `public.leads`. Self-hosting fonts (finding 8) removes Google.

### 14. Security headers and dependencies — D

- **Good:** HSTS (Vercel default, with preload), `X-Content-Type-Options`,
  `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `Referrer-Policy`, a basic `Permissions-Policy`.
  Only third parties: Google Fonts and Carto map tiles. No analytics or other
  scripts.
- **Missing:** a **Content-Security-Policy** (with `frame-ancestors 'none'`).
  The inline theme script in `index.html:51-62` needs a hash or moving to a
  file first. `X-XSS-Protection` is obsolete and can go. `public/_headers` and
  `public/_redirects` are Netlify files Vercel ignores (dead files).
- **npm audit (production):** `maplibre-gl` 5.24 **critical** (sanitizer
  bypass; low real risk because popups only show our own text, but the fix is
  v6); `react-router-dom`/`react-router` **high** (mostly server-side features
  we don't use; update anyway). Dev-only: vite, sharp and others **high**
  (build machine only; update).
- **Unused dependencies:** `leaflet` and `react-leaflet` are installed but not
  used anywhere. `react-map-gl` + `maplibre-gl` are used for 4 maps.
- **Fix:** add CSP and caching headers in `vercel.json`; remove leaflet,
  react-leaflet and the Netlify files; update react-router; decide whether
  maps earn their place (a static map image + Google Maps link would remove
  the 1 MB library and the critical advisory).

- **What:**

  - Dead code: `AnimatedCounter.jsx` (unused), `BeforeAfterSlider.jsx` (no

  - The phone number, WhatsApp number and email are typed out in about a dozen

  - Visible text lives in page components; only projects, testimonials and

  - Lint: 6 errors (known): `BeforeAfterSlider.jsx:43` (×3),

  - Docs are out of date: `CLAUDE.md` lists the old colours, Georgia font and

- **Fix:** `src/data/site.js` for contact details and shared text; one `LeadForm`

### 16. SEO and technical gaps — D

- Any unknown address (for example `/privacy`) returns **200 OK** with a "not
  found" screen; search engines may index these. `/sitemap.xml` returns the
  home page HTML instead of a sitemap.
- Heading levels skip (h1 → h3 on most pages; footer h4 after h2).
- No canonical link (matters once the company domain is connected, issue #55).
- **Fix:** add `public/sitemap.xml`, canonical tags, a proper 404 response for
  unknown paths, and correct heading levels.

### 17. Smaller accessibility issues — A (form items: C)

- Gallery photos and project-page photos open on click but can't be reached
  with the keyboard (`div onClick`, `src/pages/GalleryPage.jsx:17-20`); the
  Lightbox isn't a proper dialog (no focus trap or focus return).
- FAQ questions on Buyer Guides don't announce open/closed (`aria-expanded`).
- The "Notify me" form and guide-download forms have placeholders but no
  labels; the Ongoing search box has no label.
- Map markers are 20px; the map canvas is an unnamed landmark (axe
  `landmark-unique`).
- Keyboard use is otherwise good: skip link first, visible focus outline on
  every stop tested, dropdowns open with Enter and close with Escape, dialogs
  trap focus.

### 18. Repository history — D (for Avi)

- No keys, `.env` files, customer data or documents in the current files or
  the 78 commits on all branches I could see. Photos have no GPS/EXIF data.
- The **old admin password, its hash and the old "encryption" key** are still
  in public history (commits `4021305`, `c1e6ac9`, May 2026). The handoff
  already treats them as public. Confirm they are not used for any account
  (email, Vercel, Supabase, the future portal); history can't be un-published.

## What is already good

- **Lead security:** forms post to `/api/lead` only; it accepts POST only
  (GET returns 405, checked), validates and limits size, uses a honeypot and a
  minimum fill time, rate-limits (5 per visitor per 10 minutes, 300 per hour
  overall) and stores only a salted hash of the IP.
- **Database:** Supabase `public.leads` has RLS on with no policies, so the
  public key can't read or write it. The security advisor shows only that
  (intended) note; the performance advisor shows nothing.
- **Secrets:** `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY` and `RESEND_API_KEY` are in Vercel,
  Production only, marked Sensitive. Nothing secret in the repo.
- **Keyboard and screen-reader basics,** the 3D carousel, page titles and
  social share tags, and zero layout shift.
